**Unraid Secretary Office**

A whole team supports you in running your Unraid server: backups and restore, snapshots, a media cache for Emby, Jellyfin and Plex, the mover, a night watchman, cleanup and every log in one place — each looked after by a member of staff at a desk of their own (Mr. Backupsy, Mr. Restori, Ms. Snapshotini …), with the Team Lead, who tells you what is left to do.

It sits in Unraid's menu bar by default; ⋯ → Entry in Unraid moves it to Settings → User Utilities or to a button in Unraid's header.

Jack Streamer's tools, EmbyCache and media-disk-gather, and Ms. Moverelli's Smart Mover are by helmi1987 — thank you! A share of the tips goes to him.
