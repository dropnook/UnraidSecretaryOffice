**Unraid Secretary Office**

A small office for your Unraid server: backups and restores, snapshots, a night watch, cleanup, logs and what is where — each looked after by a member of staff at a desk of their own (Mr. Backupsy, Mr. Restori, Ms. Snapshotini …), with the Team Lead, who tells you what is left to do.

It sits in Unraid's menu bar by default; ⋯ → Entry in Unraid moves it to Settings → User Utilities or to a button in Unraid's header.

Jack Emby's tools, EmbyCache and media-disk-gather, are by helmi1987 — thank you! A share of the tips goes to him.
