**Unraid Secretary Office**

A small office for your Unraid server: snapshots, backups and restores, cleanup, logs, what is where and a night watch — each looked after by a member of staff at a desk of their own (Ms. Snapshotini, Mr. Backupsy, Ms. Dustdevil …), with a team lead who tells you what is left to do.

It sits in Unraid's menu bar by default; ⋯ → Entry in Unraid moves it to Settings → User Utilities or to a button in Unraid's header.

Jack Emby's tools, EmbyCache and media-disk-gather, are by helmi1987 — thank you! A share of the tips goes to him.
